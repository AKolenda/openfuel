Prices are unverified

Prices come from people reporting what they saw at the pump. OpenFuel doesn't check them, and some will be out of date or wrong. Always confirm the price at the pump before you fill up.

Station details come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete, closed or misplaced. Distances are straight-line, not driving distances.

Sharing a price

  • Only report a price you have just seen at that station.
  • Don't submit false prices, automate reports or try to get around the reporting limits.
  • OpenFuel may remove reports or limit reporting to keep the prices useful.

A report is public as soon as you share it. By sharing one, you agree that OpenFuel publishes it, including in the daily open data, under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0), which lets anyone use, share and adapt it. Your installation ID is never published; see the privacy page.

Using the data

For bulk use, download the open data files rather than calling the live API. The API has daily limits and may block heavy automated use.
