# OpenFuel identity proposal

The user approved the Open lane F direction. This folder retains the original review board and unused alternate; active canonical assets are in `../brand/`. Prepared with the installed frontend-design skill.

## Design plan

- Subject: an open community fuel map, used in quick decisions beside a road. The symbol needs to survive a 24 px map control and a small Android home-screen icon.
- Colour: existing Forest #285B43; Deep forest #173E2B; White #FFFFFF; Pale green #EAF1E8; occasional Road amber #EAAE38. Amber is supporting wayfinding colour, not part of the primary mark.
- Type: a lowercase wordmark drawn from Noto Sans Display SemiBold, with tight spacing and the existing simple name. Display examples are outlined vectors; no font files are shipped.
- Layout: one large recommended wordmark, followed by small-size and app-icon proofs; the alternate gets one compact comparison area. Align names and explanatory copy to the left.
- Principle: one recognisable letterform, not a collage of pump, droplet and map-pin symbols. The shape should work in one colour before any app treatments.

## Review before drawing

A broken circular O alone was too close to a generic location or loading icon. The recommendation therefore uses a compact F with a broad, curved open turn: a fuel initial that also resembles a road joining two exits. It has only one silhouette and an open interior, so it remains legible at small sizes. The open O is retained only as the quieter alternate for comparison.

## Recommended: Open lane

A broad F with a rounded entrance, two clear exits, and a short lower stem. It reads as the initial of Fuel immediately, while its open corner gives it a directional character. Keep the forest green already present in the product. The bold white mark is centred in the safe area of the Android icon; the same shape works unboxed in headers and map controls.

## Alternate: Open loop

An angular, open O shaped from one continuous route. It makes the “open” part of the name more literal, with less emphasis on fuel. This is the quieter option; the recommended F is easier to identify quickly at 24 px.

## Deliverables and scope

- `open-lane-mark.svg`: recommended one-colour mark.
- `open-lane-wordmark-light.svg` and `open-lane-wordmark-dark.svg`: outlined wordmarks.
- `open-lane-app-icon.svg`: square Android icon composition with a generous safe area.
- `open-loop-mark.svg` and `open-loop-app-icon.svg`: alternate route mark.
- `index.html`: responsive concept board with light/dark, app-icon, map-marker and 24 px examples.
- `concept-board.png`: visual review export.
